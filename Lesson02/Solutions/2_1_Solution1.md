## Problem 2.1.1
A school principal wants to know how satisfied students are with the cafeteria food. She surveys the first 50 students who enter the cafeteria during lunch on Monday.

### Solution
- __Population__: All students at the school
- __Sampling Method__: Convenience sampling (students easily accessible at lunch)
- __Is it a good method?__ No. 
    - Explanation: It only surveys students who eat lunch at that time and arrive early, which may not represent all students’ opinions about the cafeteria.
