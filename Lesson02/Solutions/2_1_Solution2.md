## Problem 2.1.2
A city council is studying public opinion about building a new park. They randomly select 200 residents from a list of all registered voters and send them a survey.

### Solution
- __Population__: All residents in the city
- __Sampling Method__: Simple random sampling
- __Is it a good method?__ Yes (generally). 
    - Explanation: Random selection helps reduce bias and gives each resident an equal chance of being chosen. However, it may miss non-registered voters.
