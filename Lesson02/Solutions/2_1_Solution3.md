## Problem 2.1.3
A fitness company wants to know how often people exercise. They post a survey on their social media page and analyze the responses they receive.

### Solution
- __Population__: All people (or possibly all adults, depending on intent) the company wants information about
- __Sampling Method__: Voluntary response sampling
- __Is it a good method?__ No. 
    - Explanation: People who choose to respond may have stronger opinions or already be interested in fitness, leading to biased results.
