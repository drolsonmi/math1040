## Problem 2.1.4
A researcher is studying study habits at a university. She divides students into groups based on their year (freshman, sophomore, junior, senior) and randomly selects 25 students from each group.

### Solution
- __Population__: All students at the university
- __Sampling Method__: Stratified random sampling (grouped by year, then randomly sampled)
- __Is it a good method?__ Yes
    - Explanation: It ensures representation from each class year, making the sample more representative of the entire student population.
