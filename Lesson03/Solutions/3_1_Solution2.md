## 3.1 Observational Studies - Solution for Practice 2
Which candidate do you support? A party wants to determine the support they have, so they send surveys to ask people of their preferred candidate

#### Potential Problems to candidate support observational study
The candidacy support study could be influenced by region. Many urban areas tend to lean democratic, and rural areas lean republican.

Age may also have an affect, as might family status

#### Potential Solutions to candidate support observational study
Collect information on the following variables:
* Candidate preference
* Living area (rural, suburb, urban)
* Age
* Married (yes with children, yes but no children, no)
