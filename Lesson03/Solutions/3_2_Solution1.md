## 3.2 Experimental Studies - Solution for Practice 1
A pharmaceutical company wants to test whether a new pain reliever reduces headache symptoms faster than a standard over-the-counter drug. They randomly assign 200 headache patients to receive either the new drug or the existing drug, then measure how many minutes it takes for symptoms to subside.

### Solution
There are many ways to establish a study that will answer this question. Below is one possible solution.
- __Treatment__: The new pain reliever drug
- __Control Group__: The patients who receive the standard (existing) over-the-counter drug
- __Treatment Group__: The patients who receive the new pain reliever
- __Explanatory Variable__: Which drug was administered (new drug vs. standard drug)
- __Response Variable__: Number of minutes until headache symptoms subside
