## 3.2 Experimental Studies - Solution for Practice 2
An agricultural researcher wants to know if a new fertilizer increases corn yield. They randomly assign 30 plots of farmland to receive either the new fertilizer or no fertilizer, then measure the bushels of corn harvested per acre at the end of the season.

### Solution
There are many ways to establish a study that will answer this question. Below is one possible solution.
- __Treatment__: Application of the new fertilizer
- __Control Group__: The plots that receive no fertilizer
- __Treatment Group__: The plots that receive the new fertilizer
- __Explanatory Variable__: Whether or not the new fertilizer was applied
- __Response Variable__: Corn yield in bushels per acre
