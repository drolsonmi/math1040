## 3.3 Blind Studies - Solution for Practice 1
A pharmaceutical company wants to test whether a new pain reliever reduces headache symptoms faster than a standard over-the-counter drug. They randomly assign 200 headache patients into two groups. One group receives the new drug; the other group receives a pill that looks identical but contains no active ingredient. Neither the patients nor the doctors administering the pills know which pill each patient receives. They measure how many minutes it takes for each patient's symptoms to subside.

Identify the placebo, explain what type of blind study this is, and describe why this design is important here.

## Solution
There are many ways to establish a study that will answer this question. Below is one possible solution.
- __Placebo__: The pill that looks identical to the new drug but contains no active ingredient.
- __Type of Blind Study__: This is a double-blind study — neither the patients (subjects) nor the doctors (researchers administering the treatment and measuring results) know which group each patient is in.
