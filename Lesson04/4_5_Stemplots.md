# Lesson 4.3 Stem-and-leaf plots (stemplots)
## Reading
Reading sections are from the [Introductory Statistics Textbook](../Resources/OpenIntroTextbook.pdf)
* 2.1.2 Stem-and-leaf plots and dot plots (pages 48-49)

## Lesson
Like a dotplot, *a __stem-and-leaf plot__ (or a stemplot for short) is used to look at the frequency of a single quantitative variable.* The difference is that instead of looking at the count of one value, we are looking at the count of values in a group of 10 (count of values between 0-9, 10-19, 20-29, 30-39, etc.).
