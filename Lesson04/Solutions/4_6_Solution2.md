## 4.6 Scatterplots - Solution for Practice 2
 
2. For each pair of variables below, state whether you would expect a positive association, a negative association, or no association, and explain why.
  - A person's age and their resting heart rate
  - The outdoor temperature and the number of hot cocoas sold at a stand
  - A person's shoe size and their favorite color

### Solution
 
- **A person's age and their resting heart rate**: generally a **negative** association. Resting heart rate tends to be higher in young children and tends to settle into a lower, steadier rate in healthy adults, so as age increases (through childhood into adulthood), resting heart rate tends to decrease somewhat.
- **The outdoor temperature and the number of hot cocoas sold at a stand**: a **negative** association. As the temperature drops, more people want a warm drink, so colder temperatures are associated with higher hot cocoa sales.
- **A person's shoe size and their favorite color**: **no association**. There's no reasonable mechanism connecting the size of someone's feet to which color they prefer — these two variables aren't related.
