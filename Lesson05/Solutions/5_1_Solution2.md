## 5.1 What is a Histogram? - Solution for Practice 2

2. A researcher collects two datasets: (1) the favorite genre of music for 200 students, and (2) the height (in inches) of those same 200 students. Which dataset should be displayed with a bar graph, and which should be displayed with a histogram? Explain your reasoning.

### Solution

- **Favorite genre of music** is a **categorical** variable. Each student picks a genre from a list of options (pop, rock, country, etc.), and those categories have no natural numerical order. This dataset should be displayed with a **bar graph**, with a gap between each bar to show that the categories are separate and could be listed in any order.
- **Height** is a **quantitative** variable. It is measured on a numerical scale, and every student has a specific numerical value. This dataset should be displayed with a **histogram**, with the heights grouped into bins and the bars touching to show that we are looking at a continuous number line.

A good way to double check yourself: ask whether the values could logically be placed in numerical order. Heights can be sorted from shortest to tallest, but genres of music can't be "sorted" in a meaningful numerical way - that's the sign that one is quantitative and the other is categorical.
