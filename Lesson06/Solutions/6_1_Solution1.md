## 6.1 Mean - Solution for Practice 1

1. Find the mean of the following dataset: \[4, 8, 6, 5, 3, 7\]

### Solution

Add all the values together, then divide by the number of values ($n = 6$):

$$\bar{x} = \dfrac{4+8+6+5+3+7}{6} = \dfrac{33}{6} = 5.5$$

The mean of the dataset is **5.5**.
