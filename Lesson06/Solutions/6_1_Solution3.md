## 6.1 Mean - Solution for Practice 3

3. The mean score on 5 tests is 84. Four of the five scores are 78, 90, 85, and 82. What was the fifth score?
{:start="3"}

### Solution

Since the mean is the sum of all values divided by $n$, we can work backwards. If the mean of 5 scores is 84, then the total of all 5 scores must be:

$$\text{Sum} = \bar{x} \cdot n = 84 \cdot 5 = 420$$

The sum of the four known scores is:

$$78 + 90 + 85 + 82 = 335$$

So the fifth score is:

$$420 - 335 = 85$$

The fifth score was **85**.
