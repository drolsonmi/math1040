## 6.2 Mode - Solution for Practice 1

1. Find the mode of the following dataset: \[3, 7, 7, 9, 10, 7, 12\]

### Solution

Count how many times each value appears:

- 3 appears 1 time
- 7 appears 3 times
- 9 appears 1 time
- 10 appears 1 time
- 12 appears 1 time

The value 7 appears more often than any other value, so the mode is **7**.
