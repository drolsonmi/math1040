## 6.2 Mode - Solution for Practice 2

1. Find the mode of the following dataset: \[2, 4, 6, 8, 10\]

### Solution

Count how many times each value appears:

- 2 appears 1 time
- 4 appears 1 time
- 6 appears 1 time
- 8 appears 1 time
- 10 appears 1 time

Every value appears exactly once, so **there is no mode**. Not every dataset has a mode — a mode only exists when at least one value repeats more often than the rest.
