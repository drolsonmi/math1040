## 6.2 Mode - Solution for Practice 3

3. Find the mode(s) of the following dataset: \[5, 8, 8, 10, 12, 12, 15\]
{:start="3"}

### Solution

Count how many times each value appears:

- 5 appears 1 time
- 8 appears 2 times
- 10 appears 1 time
- 12 appears 2 times
- 15 appears 1 time

Both 8 and 12 appear more often (twice each) than any other value, so this dataset has two modes. This is called a **bimodal** dataset, and the mode is \(\{8, 12\}\).
