## 6.3 Median - Solution for Practice 1

1. Find the median of the following dataset: \[23, 19, 45, 10, 31\]

### Solution

First, order the dataset from smallest to largest:

$$[10, 19, 23, 31, 45]$$

Since there are 5 values (an odd number), the median is the single middle value — the 3rd value in the ordered list:

$$[10, 19, \mathbf{23}, 31, 45]$$

The median is **23**.
