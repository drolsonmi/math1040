## 8.3 Correlation - Solution for Practice 3

1. For each correlation coefficient below, describe the direction (positive or negative) and strength (weak, moderate, or strong) of the relationship:
  - $r = 0.85$
  - $r = -0.42$
  - $r = 0.05$
  - $r = -0.95$

### Solution

Using the sign of $r$ for direction and $|r|$ for strength (weak: 0.00-0.29, moderate: 0.30-0.69, strong: 0.70-1.00):

- **$r = 0.85$**: Positive direction. $|r| = 0.85$ falls in the strong range. This is a **strong positive** relationship.
- **$r = -0.42$**: Negative direction. $|r| = 0.42$ falls in the moderate range. This is a **moderate negative** relationship.
- **$r = 0.05$**: Positive direction. $|r| = 0.05$ falls in the weak range. This is a **weak positive** relationship — essentially little to no meaningful linear relationship.
- **$r = -0.95$**: Negative direction. $|r| = 0.95$ falls in the strong range. This is a **strong negative** relationship.
