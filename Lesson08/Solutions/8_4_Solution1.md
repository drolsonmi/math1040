## 8.4 The Coefficient of Determination - Solution for Practice 1

1. In 8.3, we found that the correlation coefficient for the practice sets vs. quiz score data was $r = 0.9$. Find the coefficient of determination, $r^2$, and interpret it in the context of this problem.

### Solution

$$r^2 = (0.9)^2 = 0.81$$

**Interpretation**: About **81%** of the variation in quiz scores can be explained by the linear relationship with the number of practice sets completed. The remaining 19% of the variation in quiz scores is due to other factors not captured by this model, such as differences in prior knowledge, effort on the quiz itself, or random variation from one quiz to the next.
