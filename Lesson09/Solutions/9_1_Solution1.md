## 9.1 Probability - Solution for Practice 1

1. A fair six-sided die is rolled once. Find the probability of rolling an **even number**.

### Solution

**Step 1: Identify the sample space.**

$$\{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6\}$$

This sample space has 6 equally likely outcomes.

**Step 2: Identify the outcomes that satisfy the event "even number."**

$$\{2, 4, 6\}$$

This event has 3 outcomes.

**Step 3: Apply the probability formula.**

$$P(\text{even}) = \frac{\text{number of outcomes in the event}}{\text{number of outcomes in the sample space}} = \frac{3}{6} = \frac{1}{2} = 0.5 = 50\%$$

The probability of rolling an even number is **0.5, or 50%**.
