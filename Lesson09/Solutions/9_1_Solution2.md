## 9.1 Probability - Solution for Practice 2

1. A standard deck of 52 playing cards has 4 suits (hearts, diamonds, clubs, spades) with 13 cards in each suit. One card is drawn at random. Find the probability that the card is a **heart**.

### Solution

**Step 1: Identify the sample space.**

The deck has 52 cards total, so the sample space has 52 equally likely outcomes.

**Step 2: Identify the outcomes that satisfy the event "heart."**

There are 13 hearts in the deck, so this event has 13 outcomes.

**Step 3: Apply the probability formula.**

$$P(\text{heart}) = \frac{13}{52} = \frac{1}{4} = 0.25 = 25\%$$

The probability of drawing a heart is **0.25, or 25%**.
