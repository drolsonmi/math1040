## 9.1 Probability - Solution for Practice 3

1. A bag contains 5 red marbles, 3 blue marbles, and 2 green marbles. One marble is drawn at random. Find the probability that the marble is **blue**.

### Solution

**Step 1: Identify the sample space.**

The bag has $5 + 3 + 2 = 10$ marbles total, so the sample space has 10 equally likely outcomes.

**Step 2: Identify the outcomes that satisfy the event "blue."**

There are 3 blue marbles in the bag, so this event has 3 outcomes.

**Step 3: Apply the probability formula.**

$$P(\text{blue}) = \frac{3}{10} = 0.3 = 30\%$$

The probability of drawing a blue marble is **0.3, or 30%**.
