## Practice Question 9.2.2
You have a large bag of colored marbles. You randomly grab a handful of marbles and count the colors. Calculate the relative frequency of each color:

| Color | Red   | Blue  | Green | Yellow | Black | White |
| :---- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :----: | :---: | :---: |
| Count | 22    | 13    | 10    | 9      | 7     | 18    |

### Answer to Practice Question 9.2.2
There are a total of 79 marbles.

| Color  | Count | Relative Frequency    |
| :----- | :---: | :-------------------- |
| Red    | 22    | 22/79 = 0.278 = 27.8% |
| Blue   | 13    | 13/79 = 0.165 = 16.5% |
| Green  | 10    | 10/79 = 0.127 = 12.7% |
| Yellow | 9     | 9/79 = 0.114 = 11.4%  |
| Black  | 7     | 7/79 = 0.089 = 8.9%   |
| White  | 18    | 18/79 = 0.228 = 22.8% |
