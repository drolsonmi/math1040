## Practice Question 9.2.3
A teacher recorded the number of books read by students in five different genres over the course of a semester. The data is shown below:

| Genre | Fiction | Non-Fiction | Mystery | Science Fiction | Biography |
| :---- | :-----: | :---------: | :-----: | :-------------: | :-------: |
| Count | 24      | 18          | 12      | 6               | 10        |

### Answer to Practice Question 9.2.3
There are a total of 70 books read.

| Genre           | Count | Relative Frequency    |
| :-------------- | :---: | :-------------------- |
| Fiction         | 24    | 24/70 = 0.343 = 34.3% |
| Non-Fiction     | 18    | 18/70 = 0.257 = 25.7% |
| Mystery         | 12    | 12/70 = 0.171 = 17.1% |
| Science Fiction | 6     | 6/70 = 0.086 = 8.6%   |
| Biography       | 10    | 10/70 = 0.143 = 14.3% |
