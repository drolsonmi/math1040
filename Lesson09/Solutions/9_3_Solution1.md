## 9.3 Complements - Solution for Practice 1

1. A jar contains 4 red marbles, 6 blue marbles, and 10 green marbles. One marble is drawn at random. Use the complement rule to find the probability that the marble is **not red**.

### Solution

**Step 1: Find $P(\text{red})$.**

The jar has $4 + 6 + 10 = 20$ marbles total, and 4 of them are red.

$$P(\text{red}) = \frac{4}{20} = 0.2$$

**Step 2: Apply the complement rule.**

$$P(\text{not red}) = 1 - P(\text{red}) = 1 - 0.2 = 0.8 = 80\%$$

The probability that the marble is **not red** is **0.8, or 80%**.

Notice this matches what we'd get by counting directly: there are $6 + 10 = 16$ marbles that aren't red, out of 20 total, giving $\frac{16}{20} = 0.8$ as well.
