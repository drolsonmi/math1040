## 9.3 Complements - Solution for Practice 2

1. A survey finds that the probability a randomly selected person prefers coffee over tea is $P(\text{coffee}) = 0.62$. Find the probability that a randomly selected person does **not** prefer coffee.

### Solution

This problem gives us $P(\text{coffee})$ directly, so we can apply the complement rule right away — no need to count outcomes from a sample space.

$$P(\text{not coffee}) = 1 - P(\text{coffee}) = 1 - 0.62 = 0.38 = 38\%$$

The probability that a randomly selected person does **not** prefer coffee is **0.38, or 38%**.
