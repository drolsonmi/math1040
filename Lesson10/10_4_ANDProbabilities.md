## Lesson
Rearranging the conditional probability equation, we find a way to calculate "AND" probabilities:

$$P(B|A) = \frac{P(A~AND~B)}{P(A)} \qquad\to\qquad P(A~AND~B)=P(A)\cdot P(B|A)$$
