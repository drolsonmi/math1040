## 10.2 Mutually Exclusive Events - Solution for Practice 2

1. A fair six-sided die is rolled once. Find $P(\text{rolling a 1 OR rolling a 6})$.

### Solution

A single roll of the die can only show one number, so "rolling a 1" and "rolling a 6" cannot happen at the same time — these events are **mutually exclusive**.

$$P(\text{1 or 6}) = P(1) + P(6) = \frac{1}{6} + \frac{1}{6} = \frac{2}{6} = \frac{1}{3} \approx 0.333$$

The probability of rolling a 1 or a 6 is approximately **0.333, or 33.3%**.
