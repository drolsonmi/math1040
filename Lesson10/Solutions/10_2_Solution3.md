## 10.2 Mutually Exclusive Events - Solution for Practice 3

1. For each pair of events below, determine whether they are mutually exclusive. Explain your reasoning for each.
  - Drawing a card that is a Heart, or drawing a card that is a King.
  - Rolling an even number on a die, or rolling an odd number on a die.
  - A randomly selected student owns a car, or that same student has a part-time job.

### Solution

**Heart or King**: **Not mutually exclusive.** The King of Hearts is a single card that belongs to both categories at once, so there is overlap between the two events.

**Even or Odd (on a die)**: **Mutually exclusive.** Every number on a die is either even or odd, but never both at the same time. There is no overlap, so a single roll can never satisfy both events simultaneously.

**Owns a car, has a part-time job**: **Not mutually exclusive.** A student can own a car *and* have a part-time job at the same time — in fact, our survey table from 10.1 showed exactly this: 60 out of 200 students fall into both categories at once, which means there is overlap between the two events.
