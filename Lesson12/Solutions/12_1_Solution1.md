## 12.1 Probability Distributions - Solution for Practice 1
Here is a probability distribution with one missing value. Determine what that value should be.

| Category    |   A   |   B   |   C   |   D   |   E   |   F   |
| :---------- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| Probability | 0.08  | 0.16  | 0.22  | 0.34  | 0.18  |   x   |

### Answer to Practice Question 9.2.1
All probabilities have to add up to 1. So,
$$\begin{align*}
  0.08+0.16+0.22+0.34+0.18+x &= 1 \\
  0.98+x &= 1 \\
  x &= 1 - 0.98 \\
  x &= 0.02
\end{align*}$$

So, $$P(F) = 0.02$$.
