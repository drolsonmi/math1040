## 12.2 Distributions - Solution for Practice 1

1. A fair six-sided die is rolled 600 times, and the number of times each face appears is recorded. Which distribution shape does this data best represent? Explain your reasoning.

### Solution

Each face was rolled close to the same number of times (all counts fall between 97 and 103, out of an expected 100 per face if the die were rolled exactly evenly). No single face was rolled noticeably more or less often than the others, and there's no tapering off toward one end or a clear peak in the middle.

This is the hallmark of a **uniform distribution**: every outcome is roughly equally likely. This makes sense here, since a fair die is designed so that each face has exactly the same $\frac{1}{6}$ probability of appearing on any given roll — the small differences we see (98 vs. 103, etc.) are just due to natural random variation, not any underlying pattern favoring one face over another.
