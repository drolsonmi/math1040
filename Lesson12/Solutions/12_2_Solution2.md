## 12.2 Distributions - Solution for Practice 2

1. A college records the number of hours students spend studying per week. Most students study somewhere around 10 hours per week, but a small number of students study 25-30+ hours per week, stretching the data out toward the high end. What shape would you expect this distribution to have? Explain your reasoning.

### Solution

Most of the data is clustered around a typical value (10 hours), with a smaller group of students stretching the data out toward much larger values (25-30+ hours). This "stretched tail" toward the high end is the defining feature of a **right-skewed distribution**.

Remember: a distribution is named for the direction of its *tail*, not the direction where most of the data sits. Since the long tail here points toward the larger values (to the right on a number line), this distribution is skewed **right**, even though the bulk of the data (most students) is bunched up on the lower end.
