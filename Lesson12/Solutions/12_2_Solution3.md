## 12.2 Distributions - Solution for Practice 3

1. For each variable described below, identify whether you would expect its distribution to be uniform, normal, skewed left, or skewed right. Explain your reasoning for each.
  - The height of adult women in the United States.
  - The number on a randomly selected ping-pong ball drawn from a raffle drum numbered 1 through 50.
  - The age at which people retire, where most people retire around 65, but a smaller number retire much earlier (in their 40s or 50s).

### Solution

**Height of adult women**: **Normal.** Height clusters symmetrically around a typical value (roughly 5'4" in the U.S.), with fewer people the farther you get from that average in either direction, and roughly equal numbers of unusually short and unusually tall women. This is the classic bell-shaped, symmetric pattern of a normal distribution.

**Numbered ping-pong ball (1-50)**: **Uniform.** If the raffle drum is fair, every number from 1 to 50 has exactly the same chance of being drawn. There's no reason any particular number would come up more often than any other, so this matches a uniform distribution.

**Age at retirement**: **Skewed left.** Most people retire around a typical age (65), but a smaller group retires much earlier — stretching the tail of the distribution toward the *lower* values (to the left on a number line). Since the long tail points left, this distribution is skewed **left**, even though most of the data (most retirees) clusters on the higher end, near 65.
