## 15.1 Discrete and Continuous Distributions - Solution for Practice 2
2. The amount of rainfall (in inches) a city receives during the month of March.

### Solution

This variable is **continuous**. Rainfall is measured, not counted, and it can take on any value within a range — 2 inches, 2.15 inches, 2.1538... inches, and so on, with no natural "gaps" between possible values. Because it can be measured to any level of precision, it is a continuous variable.
