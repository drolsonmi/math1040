## 15.2 The Normal Distribution - Solution for Practice 1
1. IQ scores are approximately normally distributed with a mean of $\mu = 100$ and a standard deviation of $\sigma = 15$. Find the z-score for a person with an IQ of 125.

### Solution

$$z=\frac{x-\mu}{\sigma}=\frac{125-100}{15}=\frac{25}{15}\approx 1.67$$

A person with an IQ of 125 has a z-score of about **1.67**, meaning their IQ is about 1.67 standard deviations above the mean.
