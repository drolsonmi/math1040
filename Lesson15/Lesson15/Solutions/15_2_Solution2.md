## 15.2 The Normal Distribution - Solution for Practice 2
2. The heights of adult men are approximately normally distributed with $\mu = 69$ inches and $\sigma = 2.8$ inches. Find the z-score for a man who is 64 inches tall.

### Solution

$$z=\frac{x-\mu}{\sigma}=\frac{64-69}{2.8}=\frac{-5}{2.8}\approx -1.79$$

A man who is 64 inches tall has a z-score of about **-1.79**, meaning his height is about 1.79 standard deviations *below* the mean.
