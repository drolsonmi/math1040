## 15.2 The Normal Distribution - Solution for Practice 3
3. Systolic blood pressure is approximately normally distributed with $\mu = 120$ and $\sigma = 12$. Find the z-score for a reading of 138, and interpret what this z-score means.

### Solution

$$z=\frac{x-\mu}{\sigma}=\frac{138-120}{12}=\frac{18}{12}=1.5$$

A systolic blood pressure reading of 138 has a z-score of **1.5**. This means the reading is 1.5 standard deviations *above* the average systolic blood pressure — higher than average, but not extreme enough (by itself) to be flagged as an outlier using the $|z|>2$ or $|z|>3$ guidelines.
