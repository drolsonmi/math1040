## 15.2 The Normal Distribution - Solution for Practice 4
4. Using the IQ distribution ($\mu = 100$, $\sigma = 15$), between what two values do about 95% of IQ scores fall?

### Solution

By the Empirical Rule, about 95% of values fall within **2 standard deviations** of the mean:

$$\mu - 2\sigma = 100 - 2(15) = 70 \qquad \mu + 2\sigma = 100 + 2(15) = 130$$

About 95% of IQ scores fall between **70 and 130**.
