## 15.2 The Normal Distribution - Solution for Practice 5
5. Using the same IQ distribution, estimate the percentage of people with an IQ **above 130**.

### Solution

An IQ of 130 is exactly 2 standard deviations above the mean ($z = 2$). By the Empirical Rule, 95% of values fall within 2 standard deviations of the mean, leaving $100\% - 95\% = 5\%$ split evenly between the two tails:

$$\frac{100\%-95\%}{2}=\frac{5\%}{2}=2.5\%$$

About **2.5%** of people have an IQ above 130.
