## 15.3 Probability from a Normal Distribution - Solution for Practice 1
1. Tire lifespan is approximately normally distributed with $\mu = 50{,}000$ miles and $\sigma = 4{,}000$ miles. Find the probability that a randomly selected tire lasts **less than 45,000 miles**.

### Solution

**Step 1: Find the z-score.**

$$z=\frac{45{,}000-50{,}000}{4{,}000}=\frac{-5{,}000}{4{,}000}=-1.25$$

**Step 2: Use the Z-table (or technology) to find the area to the left of $z=-1.25$.**

$$P(z<-1.25)=0.1056$$

The probability that a randomly selected tire lasts less than 45,000 miles is about **0.1056, or 10.56%**.
