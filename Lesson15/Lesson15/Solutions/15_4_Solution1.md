## 15.4 Finding Z-scores from a Probability - Solution for Practice 1
1. SAT Math scores are approximately normally distributed with $\mu = 500$ and $\sigma = 100$. Find the score that marks the **90th percentile**.

### Solution

**Step 1: Find the z-score with 0.90 area to its left.**

Looking inside the Z-table for the area closest to 0.9000, we find

$$z\approx 1.28$$

**Step 2: Convert the z-score to a data value.**

$$x=\mu+z\cdot\sigma=500+(1.28)(100)=500+128=628$$

A score of about **628** marks the 90th percentile.
