## 17.2 Statistics of Sampling Distributions - Solution for Practice 1
1. A population has a mean of $\mu = 50$ and a standard deviation of $\sigma = 8$. A random sample of size $n = 16$ is taken. Find $\mu_{\bar{x}}$ and $\sigma_{\bar{x}}$.

### Solution

**Sampling mean:**

The mean of the sampling distribution is always equal to the population mean.

$$\mu_{\bar{x}} = \mu = 50$$

**Sampling standard deviation:**

$$\sigma_{\bar{x}} = \frac{\sigma}{\sqrt{n}} = \frac{8}{\sqrt{16}} = \frac{8}{4} = 2$$

So, the sampling distribution of $\bar{x}$ has a mean of **50** and a standard deviation of **2**.
