# Lesson 17: The Central Limit Theorem
We have arrived at the __Central Limit Theorem__. I cannot define it here yet. This theorem will take some time to build up to. However, it is absolutely critical to statistics. It is what links our information that we gather from samples back to the populations they came from. It is the reason why our polls from political compaigns help us understand who is going to win elections long before the counting is completed.
