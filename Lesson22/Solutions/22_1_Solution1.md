## Practice

1. A gym wants to know if members' resting heart rate decreases after 8 weeks of a new training program. They measure the same 30 members' resting heart rate before the program begins and again after 8 weeks.

## Solution

The same 30 members are measured twice — once before and once after the training program. Each "before" measurement is naturally matched with the "after" measurement for that exact same person.

Since the two measurements come from the same individuals, this is a **dependent (paired) sample**.
