## Practice

1. A researcher wants to compare average scores on a standardized test between students who attend public school and students who attend private school. She collects scores from 50 randomly selected public school students and 50 different randomly selected private school students.

## Solution

The 50 public school students and the 50 private school students are two completely separate groups of individuals. There is no natural pairing between a specific public school student and a specific private school student.

Since the two groups consist of different, unrelated individuals, this is an **independent sample**.
