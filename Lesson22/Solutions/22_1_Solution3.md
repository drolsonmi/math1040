## Practice

1. A sports scientist measures the reaction time of 20 athletes using their dominant hand and then measures the same 20 athletes' reaction time using their non-dominant hand.

## Solution

The same 20 athletes are measured twice — once for their dominant hand and once for their non-dominant hand. Each dominant-hand measurement is naturally matched to the non-dominant-hand measurement of that same athlete.

Since both measurements come from the same individuals, this is a **dependent (paired) sample**.
