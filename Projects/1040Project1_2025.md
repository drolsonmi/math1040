To begin, introduce yourselves to your group! Share something about yourself and get to know your group. Then, continue on with the project.

So far, you have learned these lessons:
* Lesson 1 on types of Variables
* Lesson 2 on Sampling Methods
* Lesson 3 on Study Designs
As a group, plan a study that your group can follow to gather data to answer the question your group has selected. Create a 1- to 2-page proposal for your study. In the study, include the following:
* What is the question your group is trying to answer?
* Who is the population of your study?
* Will you do an observational or an experimental study? 
    * If it is experimental, what is the treatment?
    * Who is the control group?
    * Will there be a placebo needed?
* What information will you gather? (that is, what are the variables of your study?)
* Describe the variables 
    * Are these variables quantitative or categorical?
    * If they are quantitative, are they discrete or continuous?
    * What are the levels of measurement for each variable?

After creating your 1-2 page proposal, submit it to the [Project 1: Design the Study - Submission]() assignment.
