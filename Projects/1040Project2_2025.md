For project 2, your group is to take the data and create plots for each variable. Additionally, create a scatterplot for pairs of quantitative variables. Remember that every graph MUST have:

You can use Microsoft Excel to make the graphs, draw the graphs by hand, or you can use other software (such as Desmos) to make the graphs, as long as the graphs are made properly. As a rule of thumb to help make the best pictures, *make your graphs so that someone who sees it can understand the graph without you needing to explain it.*

As a group create 4 graphs, at least one being categorical. Then discuss what you learn from each of the 4 graphs. For example, with a scatterplot, do you see the variables increasing together, or does one increase while the other decreases? Or discuss which is the largest category in your dataset.
