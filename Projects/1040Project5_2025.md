For this project, you and your group will use the [Desmos graphing software](www.desmos.com/calculator) to plot the data and a normal distribution to match it. Here is how to create a histogram of a dataset (let's assume our dataset is {14, 15, 15, 16, 16, 17, 17, 17, 17, 18, 18, 18, 19, 19, 20, 21, 21}).

* Open [desmos.com/calculator](www.desmos.com/calculator)
* Put in your data by selecting a cell and typing "A = [14,15,15,16,16,17,17,17,17,18,18,18,19,19,20,21,21]" the press "Enter" (of course, enter your own data)
* Find the bin width using the principles from lesson 5
  * There are 17 observations here with a range of 21-14=7. So, the bin width is 17/7 = 2.43. Rounding up, our bin width is 3.
* Plot the histogram.
  * In another cell on Desmos, type "histogram(A,3)" where 3 is the bin width
  * There is an option of "BAR HEIGHTS". Select the "Relative" option.
  * There is a magnifying glass on the left bar. Click on this to zoom into the data
