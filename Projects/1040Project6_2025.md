### Common Feedback

Calculations:
* Your calculations used a critical Z score. However, we don't know the population standard deviation, so we should use a critical T score instead with the sample standard deviation.

Interpretation:
* For the interpretation, you have a general statement  of "We are 95% confident that the **true mean** is between 3.73 and 4.412." 

It is a good idea to specify this specifically. Remember that you are trying to explain this to your audience, so we want everything to be crystal clear.  I'd recommend rewriting the interpretation this way:

"We are 95% confident that **the true mean weight loss** is between 3.73 and 4.412"

* How confident are you in your interpretation? The way you stated it, it sounds like the true mean is *definitely* between those two values. However, confidence intervals only give you a probable range. There's a chance that the true average is not within the center.

So, it's good to include your confidence interval in your interpretation, such as "We are 95% confident that ..." to indicate there's a chance it isn't in that interval.
