How these are calculated

x̄ = Σx ⁄ n is the sum of the values divided by how many there are.

σ = √( Σ(x − x̄)² ⁄ n ) treats the data as the whole population, so it divides by n.

s = √( Σ(x − x̄)² ⁄ (n − 1) ) treats the data as a sample, so it divides by n − 1. It needs at least two values.

The median is the middle value of the sorted data (or the average of the two middle values). With “Median of halves,” Q1 and Q3 are the medians of the lower and upper halves, leaving out the median itself when n is odd — the method TI-83/84 calculators and most intro stats courses use. “Include median in halves” keeps it in both halves when n is odd, so results can differ slightly.
